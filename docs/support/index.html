Quick answers

Where is my data stored?

Only on this iPhone. There's no account and no server. Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Set up your new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or computer) and your calendars come along. To get Pro back, tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

How do I change a single day?

Tap the day in the calendar, then pick a different shift or add a note. Your rotation stays the same on every other day.

My rotation changed. What do I do?

Go to Settings › Rotation, set the new pattern and start day, and save. All dates are recalculated; your notes are kept.

How do I add the widget?

The widget is part of Pro. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit or +, search for On & Off and choose a size.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase, and your family can use it too with Family Sharing. After reinstalling, tap Restore Purchase.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
