2. Data stored on your iPhone

The app stores what you enter (calendars, shift types, rotations, single-day changes, notes and settings) only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. We never receive this data. A copy needed for the Home Screen widget is kept in a storage area shared only between the app and its widget on the same iPhone.

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS includes this data in your backups. Apple processes backups under its own privacy policy; we have no access to them.

You can delete all data at any time in Settings › Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

3. Sharing a month as an image

When you share a month, the image is created on your iPhone and goes only where you send it. Notes are never included. If you save the image to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos; the app cannot read your photo library.

4. Purchases

Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone whether Pro is active. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Statistics and crash reports from Apple

If you agreed in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated statistics (for example, the number of installs) and crash reports that do not identify you. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

6. Contacting us by e-mail

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message only to answer you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). E-mails started from the app include a line with the app version, iOS version and app language; you can delete it before sending. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes these e-mails under its own privacy policy. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest after two years, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
