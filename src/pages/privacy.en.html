Short version: On & Off does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

On & Off is available for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). This policy applies to both.

3. Where your data is stored

Your calendars, shifts and notes are stored only on your device. On iPhone they are included in your iCloud or computer backup if you use one. On Android they are included in your device's Google backup if backup is turned on. We have no server and cannot access your data.

On iPhone, a copy needed for the Home Screen widget is kept in a storage area shared only between the app and its widget on the same iPhone. Apple and Google process backups under their own privacy policies.

4. Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Copies in your device backup (iCloud or Google) are managed by Apple or Google according to your backup settings.

5. Sharing a month as an image

When you share a month, the image is created on your device and goes only where you send it. Notes are never included. On iPhone, if you save the image to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos; the app cannot read your photo library.

6. Purchases

On & Off Pro is sold through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The store processes your payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. We never receive your payment details, your name or your e-mail address. Apple's or Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Permissions on Android

The Android app asks for no permissions. It uses internet access only to communicate with Google Play for purchases and to open links you tap.

8. Statistics and crash reports from Apple and Google

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostic data with Apple or Google, they may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as crash rates. We cannot identify you from them.

On iPhone, Apple may also give us crash reports that do not identify you. You can change your choice in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

9. Contacting us by e-mail

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message only to answer you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). E-mails started from the app include a line with the app version, the operating system version and app language; you can delete it before sending. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes these e-mails under its own privacy policy. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed, at the latest after two years, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
